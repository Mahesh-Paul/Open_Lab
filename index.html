04 / PRODUCTION POST-MORTEM • WHAT WOULD BREAK IN REALITY
1. FX RATE TIMING DRIFT

For multi-currency cross-border transactions, the authorization FX rate locked at capture differs from the settlement FX rate applied by card schemes 48 hours later, creating continuous floating currency variance.

2. NET-SETTLED BATCHES & RESERVES

Commercial acquiring banks do not settle gross individual charges; they deposit aggregated lump-sum batches net of interchange fees, dispute fees, and rolling risk holdbacks (e.g. 5% 180-day reserves).

3. PARTIAL CAPTURES & SPLITS

In e-commerce workflows where orders ship from different warehouses, a single authorization generates multiple partial captures and subsequent partial refunds, breaking naive one-to-one joins without an event graph.
